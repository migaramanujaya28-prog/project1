Case study

BiteGo: Premium mobile delivery experience

A food delivery app for Colombo's design-conscious urbanites: warm, fast and appetite-first, with transparent order tracking.

▶ Open live demo app

Fully interactive: add dishes, filter cravings, apply the BITENEW offer and place an order.

My roleUI Designer
PlatformMobile app
MarketColombo, Sri Lanka
DeliverablesUI screens + design system

Set the visual direction

Modern tactile minimalism for Colombo's design-conscious food lovers.

  • Warm cream canvas with floating white cards
  • Spice-inspired coral accents and fresh mint highlights
  • 3D food cutouts that break card boundaries

Build the foundations

Colour, type and spacing defined before any screen.

  • Plus Jakarta Sans headlines paired with Inter body text
  • 4px spacing rhythm and 16-24px rounded corners
  • Three elevation levels, including a coral glow

Design the components

A reusable kit for consistent screens.

  • Pill buttons with coral gradient and soft glow
  • Filter chips, rating badges and dish cards
  • 52px inputs and glass floating bag bar

Compose the screens

Components assembled into a clear, appetite-first feed.

  • Hero greeting with search and cuisine chips
  • Offer banner, popular carousel and recommendations
  • Glass bottom navigation for quick access
